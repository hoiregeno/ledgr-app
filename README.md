# Ledgr

Ledgr is a clean, lightweight personal finance tracker tailored specifically for side hustlers and small businesses. Easily log your sales, manage debts, and monitor inventory all from a single, intuitive dashboard that calculates your net profit at a glance.

[Visit Ledgr](https://ledgr-png.netlify.app/)

## Features

* **Live Dashboard:** Get an instant overview of your net profit, total revenue and outstanding expenses.
* **Sales & Expense Tracking:** Log every transaction with customizable categories to keep your books balanced.
* **Inventory Management:** Keep tabs on stock levels, unit costs, and retail prices.
* **Debt Tracker:** Monitor who owes you to protect your cash flow.
* **Advanced Filters:** Easily sort through transactions by date, category or status.
* **CSV Export:** Export your financial records in seconds for taxes or deep-dive analysis.

## Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React + Vite (for lightning-fast development and builds)
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (fully responsive, clean layout)
* **Routing:** React Router DOM
* **Deployment:** Netlify
